# Brand OS Alignment — Marketing

> **How Pillar 7 (Marketing) maps to the full Brand OS.**

---

## Connection to Brand Method (Pillars 1-4: Identity)

| Pillar | Marketing Implication |
|--------|---------------------|
| **DNA** (Purpose, Vision) | Every piece must reinforce "eliminate the black hole in staffing" |
| **Positioning** | Never sound like a generic job board, ATS, or social network |
| **Narrative** | Our messaging arc: Chaos → Trust → Submission → Placement |
| **Identity** | The "Guide" archetype — confident, practical, respectful, non-buzzwordy |

## Connection to Brand Mode (Pillars 5-8: Action)

| Pillar | Marketing Implication |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Offer** | Market the 5 product motions: Connect, Discover, Submit, Trust, Place |
| **Funnel** | Content must serve acquisition (awareness), conversion (signup), and retention (engagement) |
| **Marketing** (this pillar) | Creative → Campaigns → Distribution → Optimization |
| **Operations** | Marketing execution must be trackable — every piece logged in vault |

## Connection to Brand Mind (Pillars 9-12: Intelligence)

| Pillar | Marketing Implication |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Discover** | What signals do we need? Engagement data, channel growth, submission starts |
| **Diagnose** | Why is a channel underperforming? Wrong audience, wrong message, or wrong format? |
| **Decide** | Score every marketing decision 1-5: trust, context, manual work, submissions, response, memory, placements, staffing specificity |
| **Deploy** | Ship, track, and review every campaign |

## Decision Scorecard for Marketing

Before launching any campaign or major content initiative, score 1-5:
- [ ] Does it build trust?
- [ ] Does it preserve context?
- [ ] Does it reduce manual work?
- [ ] Does it increase useful submissions?
- [ ] Does it improve response/follow-up?
- [ ] Does it create relationship memory?
- [ ] Does it support placements?
- [ ] Is it specific to US staffing?

**Score ≥ 32: Build | 24-31: Test | < 24: Park/Kill**
