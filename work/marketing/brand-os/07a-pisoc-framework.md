# PISOC Framework

> **The messaging engine behind every piece of Jobfynder communication.**
> **From Brand OS Pillar 3 (Narrative) + Pillar 7 (Marketing).**

---

## The Hierarchy

```
PROBLEM → IMPACT → SOLUTION → OUTCOME → CTA
  ↓          ↓         ↓          ↓       ↓
 What's    Why does  How do    What      What
 broken?   it hurt?  we fix    changes?  now?
                     it?
```

## Layer Breakdown

### P — Problem
- State the pain in the reader's words
- Be specific to US staffing
- Make them nod: "Yes, that's exactly what happens"
- *Bad:* "Recruiting is inefficient"
- *Good:* "You submit a candidate and never hear back — no status, no feedback, no placement"

### I — Impact
- Quantify the cost or describe daily frustration
- "This costs you..." or "This means you..."
- *Bad:* "This causes problems"
- *Good:* "Every invisible submission is a placement you could have made"

### S — Solution
- Introduce Jobfynder concretely
- What exactly does it do? Not what it "represents"
- *Bad:* "Our AI-powered platform revolutionizes staffing"
- *Good:* "Jobfynder tracks every submission from start to outcome, so you always know where things stand"

### O — Outcome
- Paint the after-picture with specifics
- What changes in their day-to-day?
- *Bad:* "Better efficiency and transparency"
- *Good:* "You'll know exactly which recruiter has your candidate, how many submissions they've made, and whether those submissions turn into placements"

### C — CTA
- One clear, low-friction action
- "→" prefix for visual cue
- *Bad:* "Learn more about our platform"
- *Good:* "→ Start your first tracked submission"

## Example — Full PISOC Message

**[P]** Every week, you send hotlists to recruiters and hear nothing back. No status, no feedback, no way to know if anyone even looked. **[I]** Those invisible submissions are placements slipping through your fingers — and you can't even count how many. **[S]** Jobfynder gives every submission a tracked trail: who received it, when they opened it, and what happened next. **[O]** So instead of wondering, you'll know exactly which recruiters actually work your candidates — and which ones don't. **→ Submit your first candidate with full tracking →**
