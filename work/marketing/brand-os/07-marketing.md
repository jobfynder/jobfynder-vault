## 1. Positioning Statement

**For** US staffing professionals (recruiters, BSRs, consultants, employers)
**Who** are drowning in fragmented signals, untrusted relationships, and black-hole submissions
**Jobfynder is** the Professional Network Built for US Staffing
**That** turns recruiting chaos into trusted outcomes
**Unlike** generic job boards, noisy social networks, or ATS clones
**We** create memory from work — so you know who to trust, what to submit, where it stands, and what happened next.

## 2. Audience Segments

| Segment | Pain Point | Our Promise | Primary Channels |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Recruiters** | Too many unverified candidates, no relationship context | Know who to trust before you submit | LinkedIn, Staffing forums, Email |
| **Bench Sales Recruiters (BSRs)** | Hotlists go into a black hole, no visibility | Every submission tracked, every outcome known | Telegram, WhatsApp, Staffing groups |
| **Consultants (Bench)** | Can't find real opportunities, ghosted by recruiters | Verified opportunities with context | LinkedIn, Telegram, Community |
| **Employers** | Too much noise, can't verify candidate quality | Context-rich submissions, not cold resumes | Direct outreach, Partnerships |

## 3. Content Pillars (The 4 Topics We Own)

1. **Trust in Staffing** — Verification, relationship intelligence, transparency
2. **Submission Workflow** — How to move from chaos to structured outcomes
3. **Placement Stories** — Real outcomes, what worked, patterns that repeat
4. **Industry Context** — US staffing trends, regulatory shifts, market intelligence
